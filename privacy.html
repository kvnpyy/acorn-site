Privacy

Your meetings stay yours.

Acorn does not require an account. Meeting audio stays on your device unless you connect your own AI provider.

What this means

You can download and use Acorn without creating an account with us. Notes, transcripts, and recordings are stored on your machine.

Meeting audio is not sent to Acorn or RepEdge. If you connect an AI provider with your own keys, the content you choose to send goes to that provider under your agreement with them.

This website is a static download page. It does not sign you in or collect meeting data. The host may keep ordinary request logs, such as IP address and the pages requested.
