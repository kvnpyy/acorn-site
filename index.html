Before you download.

What is Acorn?

Acorn is a local meeting notes app for Mac and Windows. It captures from your computer so no bot joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams. Notes stay on this machine. Live Ask answers during the meeting, using a model and keys you control. Built on Anarlog (MIT).

Does a bot join the call?

No. Acorn captures from your computer. Nobody else sees a notetaker arrive.

Where do the notes live?

On this computer. Transcripts and notes are not uploaded to us in order to take notes.

Will this run on my Mac?

Apple Silicon, macOS Sequoia 15 or later.

Will this run on Windows?

Windows 10/11 x64. Run acorn-windows.exe. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn — click More info, then Run anyway. Needs WebView2 (Windows 11 already has it).

Which meeting apps does Acorn work with?

Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or the room itself. Acorn never joins as a participant.

Do I need an AI account?

Not to capture notes. For summaries or Live Ask, connect a provider you already use, with your keys.

Can I connect Google Calendar?

Yes. It is optional. Acorn can read your Google Calendar so notes match the right meeting. Calendar data stays on your Mac. See how we handle Google user data.
